Frequently asked questions

I already have a 3D model. What happens next?

Send the model with your material, colour and quantity. We review it, then contact you on WhatsApp with the confirmed price.

I have an idea but no 3D model. Can you help?

Yes. Choose Request 3D Design and tell us what you need, with sketches or photos if you have them. We discuss the design and price on WhatsApp before anything is printed.

Which materials and colours can I choose?

PLA, PETG, ABS and TPU. Colours are white, black, blue and yellow.

Which file formats do you accept?

STL, OBJ, 3MF and STEP. If you only have a sketch or photo, we can model it for you.

How long does printing take?

It depends on the size, settings and quantity. We tell you the print time together with the price.

Do you deliver outside Colombo?

Yes, island-wide by courier in 2 to 3 days, with cash on delivery available.
