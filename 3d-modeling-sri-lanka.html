How our 3D design process works

  1. Tell us what you need. Use the design request form and attach sketches, photos, dimensions or reference files.
  2. We model it. We create the CAD model and a blueprint showing the dimensions and assembly.
  3. You approve the design. We adjust until it is right and confirm the price on WhatsApp.
  4. We print it. Your part is 3D printed in Colombo and delivered island-wide.

Frequently asked questions

Can you 3D model something from a sketch or photo?

Yes. Send a sketch, photos and the key measurements and we create the CAD model, then show you the blueprint for approval.

Can you copy a broken or discontinued part?

Yes. Send the part or clear photos with measurements and we model a printable replacement. Complex parts may need the original to measure.

How much does 3D modeling cost in Sri Lanka?

It depends on how complex the part is and how much detail you can provide. Describe your idea in the design request form and we confirm the price on WhatsApp before any work starts.

Do you also print the parts you design?

Yes. We design and print in the same workshop in Colombo, and we only print after you approve the design.

What do I need to send to get started?

A description of what the part does, rough dimensions, and any sketches, photos or reference files you have. The more detail, the more accurate the first model.
