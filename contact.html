Frequently asked questions

I already have a 3D model. What happens next?

Send the request with your material, colour and quantity. We review it, then contact you on WhatsApp with the confirmed price.

I have an idea but no 3D model. Can you help?

Yes. Choose Request 3D Design and tell us what you need, with sketches or photos. We show you the blueprint and agree the price before anything is printed.

Which materials and colours can I choose?

PLA, PETG, ABS and TPU. Common colours are white, black, red and orange.

Which file formats do you accept?

STL, OBJ, 3MF and STEP. If you only have a sketch or photo, we can model it for you.

How long does printing take?

It depends on the size, settings and quantity. We tell you the print time together with the price.

How much does 3D printing cost in Sri Lanka?

The price depends on the size of the part, the material, the print settings and the quantity. Upload your STL on the instant estimate page for a planning price in rupees, then request a final quote and we confirm the exact price on WhatsApp.

Do you offer 3D modeling and CAD design in Sri Lanka?

Yes. Send a sketch, photos, measurements or a broken part and we create a printable 3D model. You approve the design before anything is printed.

Do you sell 3D printers?

Yes. See the 3D Printers page. Add one to your cart and send the order on WhatsApp.

Do you deliver outside Colombo?

Yes, island-wide by courier. We confirm delivery time and cost when you order.
